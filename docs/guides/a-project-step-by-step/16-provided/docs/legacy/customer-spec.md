# Customer service — specification

## 1. Overview

Customer accounts, and the fraud review the risk team runs on them.

## 2. Customer accounts

A customer has an account: a name, an email, a billing address. The email of an account is unique. A customer
changes their address at any time.

## 3. Fraud review

A member of the risk team places a customer under fraud review, and lifts it. A review is lifted only by a
member of the risk team. The other services ask whether a customer is blocked: yes or no.

## 4. Data

Tables `customer_account` and `fraud_review`; identifiers are UUIDs.

## 5. Conventions

Identifiers are UUIDs, generated by the service, never by the caller.

## 6. Ruling history

- 2024-03-12 — A customer under fraud review can neither pay nor be refunded. (risk committee)
- 2024-09-02 — Reviews older than one year are archived, not deleted. (risk committee)
